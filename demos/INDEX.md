# Simulation track replays

These four MP4 videos visualize saved simulation telemetry from **R34_final10**, recorded on 31 October 2026. Red is the reference track, blue the logged flight path, and yellow the drone. Altitude and simulation time come from the original logs. Playback is approximately 5x real simulation time.

- [00 custom three section.mp4](00_custom_three_section.mp4)
- [01 six section turns.mp4](01_six_section_turns.mp4)
- [02 long hairpins.mp4](02_long_hairpins.mp4)
- [03 compact four section.mp4](03_compact_four_section.mp4)

These are historical data replays, not drone-camera recordings, hardware flights, or newly executed tests of the owner-reported Antigravity fix. They do not establish all-track correctness. The displayed results apply to those original frozen sources. Only these four runs are shown; unsuccessful runs elsewhere in project history are not thereby resolved. Exact source-CSV and video hashes are recorded in provenance.json.
